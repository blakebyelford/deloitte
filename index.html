01 / The Brief

A marketer who knows the work doesn't ship without an operator in the loop.

I'm a B2B marketing manager who runs at the seam between strategy and execution. Most of my career has been spent making sure marketing actually gets out the door: writing the brief, coordinating the agency, lining up the seller, processing the SOW, getting it through legal, hitting the date, and reporting the result.

At Life Fitness, I owned U.S. go-to-market for Hospitality and Multi-Unit Housing, two of the largest verticals in commercial fitness. I coordinated cross-functional teams across product marketing, social, content, web, and trade, managed a ~$100K working budget, and supported event programs ranging from $25K to $750K per conference.

Before that, I built the entire marketing function at a B2B SaaS startup from zero, managing agencies and offshore contractors across SEO, paid media, creative, and web development through an $18M Series A.

I treat every campaign like a backlog item. Brief it. Move it through review. Ship it. Measure it. Then make the workflow better the next time around.
